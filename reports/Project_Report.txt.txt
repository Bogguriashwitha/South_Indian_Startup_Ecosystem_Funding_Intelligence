SOUTH INDIAN STARTUP ECOSYSTEM & FUNDING INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD

Project Report



1. INTRODUCTION

The South Indian Startup Ecosystem & Funding Intelligence Dashboard is a data analytics project developed to analyze startup funding patterns and investment activity.

The project focuses on understanding funding trends across different years, cities, and startup sectors. It provides insights into total funding, number of deals, average deal size, funding concentration, year-over-year growth, and deal-size volatility.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow using Python, MySQL, Excel, and Power BI.



2. BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

The main objective of this project is to analyze startup funding data and identify meaningful patterns that can support investment and business decisions.

The analysis aims to:

• Understand yearly startup funding trends.
• Compare funding across major cities.
• Identify sectors receiving higher funding.
• Analyze the concentration of funding across sectors.
• Measure year-over-year changes in funding.
• Identify sectors with high deal-size volatility.
• Present the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.



3. DATASET DESCRIPTION

The project uses the Indian Startup Funding dataset containing startup investment records.

The original dataset contains 3,044 records and 10 columns. The data includes information such as startup name, industry vertical, subvertical, city location, investors, investment type, funding amount, date, and remarks.

After data cleaning and transformation, additional fields such as year and sector category were created for analysis.

The cleaned dataset was used for SQL analysis and Power BI dashboard development.



4. DATA CLEANING & PREPARATION

The dataset was cleaned and prepared using Python and Pandas before performing analysis.

The following steps were performed:

• Standardized column names for easier analysis.
• Converted funding amounts into numeric values.
• Converted dates into a usable date format.
• Created a Year column from the funding date.
• Standardized city names, including Bangalore to Bengaluru.
• Cleaned inconsistent city names such as Gurgaon to Gurugram.
• Standardized industry and sector names.
• Created a Sector Category column for better sector-level analysis.
• Checked for duplicate records.
• Checked for negative funding values.
• Handled missing values where appropriate.
• Saved the cleaned dataset as startup_clean.csv.

The final cleaned dataset contains 3,044 records and 12 columns.



5. SQL ANALYSIS

MySQL was used to perform structured analysis on the cleaned startup funding dataset.

The following analyses were performed:

• Yearly funding analysis
• City-wise funding analysis
• Sector-wise funding analysis
• Funding concentration analysis
• Year-over-year funding growth analysis
• Sector deal-size volatility analysis

SQL aggregation functions such as SUM(), COUNT(), AVG(), and STDDEV() were used to calculate key business metrics.

The SQL results were exported to CSV files and used for further reporting and dashboard development.



6. EXCEL ANALYSIS

Excel was used to organize and review the SQL analysis outputs.

Six analysis outputs were imported into separate Excel sheets:

• Yearly Funding
• City Funding
• Sector Funding
• Funding Concentration
• YoY Growth
• Sector Volatility

Excel was used as an intermediate reporting layer to review the results before creating the final Power BI dashboard.



7. POWER BI DASHBOARD

Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard from the cleaned startup funding dataset.

The dashboard contains three main pages:

1. Executive Overview

This page provides a high-level view of:
• Total Funding
• Total Deals
• Average Deal Size
• Total Funding by Year
• Total Funding by Sector

2. City & Sector Intelligence

This page analyzes:
• Total Funding by City
• Total Funding by Sector
• Total Deals by City
• Total Deals by Sector
• Year and City slicers for interactive filtering

3. Risk & Volatility Monitoring

This page analyzes:
• Funding Volatility by Sector
• Records with Funding Amount
• Total Funding by Trend
• Year slicer for interactive filtering

The dashboard was designed to provide a clear and interactive view of startup funding patterns.



8. KEY FINDINGS

The analysis produced the following key findings:

• Bengaluru recorded the highest total funding among the cities analyzed.
• Funding levels changed significantly across different years.
• E-Commerce was one of the major sectors by total funding.
• A large share of total funding was concentrated among a small number of sectors.
• Average deal sizes varied significantly between sectors.
• Transportation showed high average deal size and high deal-size volatility.
• Funding activity in 2020 was much lower than in previous years, with only a small number of records in the dataset.
• The results show that startup funding is concentrated in selected cities and sectors.



9. BUSINESS INSIGHTS

The analysis provides several useful business insights:

• Investors can identify cities with stronger startup funding activity.
• Sector-level analysis can help identify sectors attracting higher investment.
• Funding concentration can help investors understand where investment is heavily focused.
• Year-over-year analysis helps identify periods of funding growth and decline.
• Deal-size volatility can indicate sectors where investment amounts vary significantly.
• The dashboard can support data-driven comparison of startup markets and funding patterns.

These insights can help investors, analysts, and policymakers understand the startup funding landscape and identify areas for further investigation.



10. CONCLUSION

The South Indian Startup Ecosystem & Funding Intelligence Dashboard demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow using Python, MySQL, Excel, and Power BI.

The project transformed raw startup funding data into a cleaned and structured dataset, performed SQL-based analysis, reviewed the results using Excel, and presented key insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard provides a clear view of funding trends, city and sector performance, funding concentration, year-over-year growth, and deal-size volatility.

Overall, this project demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, data analysis, SQL, Excel, Power BI, data visualization, and business insight generation.
